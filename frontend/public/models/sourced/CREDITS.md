# Sourced 3D assets

Free models from Sketchfab, all under
[CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/): attribution is
required and commercial use is allowed. Each folder keeps the original
`license.txt`/`SOURCE.md` written by the sourcing tool.

The GLBs here are optimised copies of the originals: textures recompressed to
WebP at 1024px (no geometry compression, so no decoder is needed at runtime).
`SOURCE.md` still points at the original model.
